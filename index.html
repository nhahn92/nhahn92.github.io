Professional Self‑Assessment

My journey through the Computer Science program at Southern New Hampshire University has shaped me into a well‑rounded IT professional preparing to transition into cybersecurity. Over the past two and a half years, I have developed a strong foundation in software engineering, algorithms, databases, and secure system design. Building my ePortfolio has allowed me to reflect on this growth, demonstrate my technical abilities, and showcase how my coursework and enhancements align with my long‑term career goal: beginning my career in IT and advancing into cybersecurity as I gain experience and specialized training.

Throughout the program, I learned how interconnected the fields of software development, system administration, networking, and security truly are. Courses such as CS‑465 Full Stack Development I, CS‑340 Client/Server Development, and CS‑360 Mobile Architecture & Programming taught me how modern applications are built across multiple layers using frameworks like the MEAN stack. CS‑300 DSA: Analysis and Design and CS‑320 Software Testing, Automation QA strengthened my ability to design efficient algorithms, evaluate trade‑offs, and ensure reliability in larger systems. Courses focused on databases and security, including CS‑231 Database Systems and CS‑305 Software Security, helped me understand how data integrity, validation, and secure coding practices form the backbone of safe and maintainable applications.

These experiences directly support my career plan. I intend to begin in IT, where I can apply my troubleshooting, system analysis, and technical communication skills while gaining hands‑on experience with operating systems, networks, and support workflows. This foundation is essential for transitioning into cybersecurity, where understanding how systems operate is just as important as knowing how to secure them. My enhancements in this capstone demonstrate the skills required for both fields: secure coding, architectural design, algorithmic thinking, database integrity, and a strong security mindset.

The artifacts in my ePortfolio represent a single full‑stack application enhanced across three categories: Software Design and Engineering, Algorithms and Data Structures, and Databases. Completing this ePortfolio has helped me recognize how my coursework, projects, and enhancements fit together to form a cohesive skill set.
